Hi, I'm Favour. I like asking questions that lead to more questions.

I'm a student, reader, and independent creator with a strong interest in psychology and human behavior. I'm curious about what shapes our personalities, beliefs, relationships, and the ways we see the world. Questions about adolescence, belonging, social experiences, and identity often send me down new paths of reading and research.

I also enjoy philosophy, history, criminology, and social issues. I like looking beyond first impressions, considering different perspectives, and asking what evidence supports a claim. I don't think learning means having an instant answer. Sometimes it means admitting that a question is complicated.

Stories are another way I explore ideas. I enjoy animated television and media analysis, especially when humor or satire makes a point about society. Characters from shows like The Boondocks and SpongeBob SquarePants can be interesting starting points for questions about authority, fairness, personality, and everyday life.

When I'm not reading or writing, I like graphic design, building fictional brands and media worlds, and experimenting with creative projects. I enjoy the process of taking an idea and gradually giving it a shape, a name, or a story.

Looking ahead, I hope to study psychology and build the skills to do research and write thoughtfully about people and society. FAVOUR.exe is my personal notebook on the internet: a place to collect ideas, share reflections, and see how my thinking changes over time. It's always a work in progress—and that's the point.
