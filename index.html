HI!_
student / curious observer / aspiring psychology researcher / creative thinker

I'm Favour. I like figuring things out.

Why do people act the way they do? What shapes our beliefs? What can a television show reveal about society? And how does a person become who they are?

I'm interested in psychology, philosophy, sociology, art, design and storytelling. Some days I read and research. Other days I sketch concepts, collect ideas or just think about a question longer than I expected.

This website isn't a record of having everything figured out. It's a place to keep exploring, and to look back at how my thinking changes.
